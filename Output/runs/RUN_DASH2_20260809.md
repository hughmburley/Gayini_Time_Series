## Overrides — four, each stated

**1 · §3.2 not applied.** The spec asked for the "Where it sits" y-axis to become *"Share
of cells wet, mean over years (%)"* per AZ/CX. **It plots
`flood_frequency_pct = 100 × wet_years / valid_years` at plot support** — a genuine
between-year frequency, already correctly labelled *"Annual wet freq. (% of years)"*.
**Applying the AZ/CX wording would have introduced the very error AZ exists to prevent,
in reverse.** Y-axis left alone; §3.6's p-value removal applied.

**2 · §3.3 verified, x-axis left alone — as the spec allowed.** The response panel draws
per-cell `flood_freq_pct` from a census context counted on the **8058 stack**, and its
axis already reads *"How often it floods · between-year flood frequency (%)"*. Correct as
it stands. **No relabel.**

**3 · §3a fallback taken.** One marker per community above 10% share was not implemented;
the sheets keep the single marker with *"shown on Inland Floodplain (wet) — 33% of this
paddock"* on the panel face, which is the fallback the spec pre-registered. The
multi-marker version needs the panel's GAM cloud rebuilt per community and could not be
fitted into this pass without reworking `gayini_veg_water_census_panels.R`, which §1
forbids.

**4 · §5's registrar not called directly.** `gayini_write_and_register_figure()` performs
its own `ggsave` with the **default device**, while the dashboards render through
`ragg::agg_png`. Routing the composed sheet through it would change the renderer and so
the sheet's appearance, which §1 forbids. **The write stays with `gayini_save_figure()`
and `gayini_dash2_register()` does the register half**, reusing `gayini_sha256_first50()`
and the identical `INSERT OR REPLACE` column set, called inside
`gayini_build_dashboard()` immediately after the write — **so a v2 sheet still cannot
exist unregistered.**

## Defects found in my own work, and fixed

1. **The response panel fell back to the legacy plot-support panel** on the first run,
   because `ctx$census` was never attached. §3a's corrections would have been applied to
   the wrong panel entirely. Caught by reading the rendered sheet — the footnote said
   *"n = 1 plot"*. Fixed by attaching `gayini_census_context()` as the v1 driver does.
2. **`Bala 8/11` was silently skipped.** Reconstructing paddock names from filename slugs
   turned `Bala_8_11` into "Bala 8 11", which matches no zone. **Fixed in the robust
   direction — slugify the zone names and match those** — and the driver now hard-stops
   on any unmatched slug rather than skipping.
3. **`labs(y=)` lost to a scale name**, so the first y-axis relabel did nothing. The scale
   is now mutated in place, preserving its limits and expansion.
4. **`labs(caption=)` on the map was inert** — that panel is a cowplot composite. The
   checkerboard note moved to the sheet header, where it renders.
5. **Two lines overflowed the sheet's right edge** and are now wrapped.
