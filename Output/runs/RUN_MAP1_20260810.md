## 3 · Overrides

**O1 · No locator inset, on any of the four.** §7 requires one. All four locator paths in
this codebase exist to place a **sub-unit inside the property**; these maps **are** the
property, so such an inset would locate the property inside itself, and a regional inset
would need boundary data the project does not hold. The property boundary is the frame
instead. **No locator path was parameterised** — which is what §7's warning is actually
protecting against.

**O2 · Transparency encodes size, not inclusion, on M1.** The spec asks for the 18
under-floor areas in "community hue, heavily lightened". Mapping the lighten to the
**500-cell threshold applied to all 156** is the same rendering and a truer legend: eight
of the not-analysed areas are also under the floor, and they are lightened too because
that is what the column says.

**O3 · The not-analysed areas are drawn as context on M2.** Not in the spec. Without them
M2 had large white voids a reader could not identify, and the legend carried a swatch with
no data behind it.

## 4 · Disagreements

**D1 · §2's stated input for the 156 areas does not exist.** The spec sources them from
`Gayini_Results.gpkg`. That file holds `management_zones` (64) and `vegetation_units`
(20) — **no paddock × community layer**. The only part polygons in the repo are
`PARTREG_part_residuals.gpkg` (**115**, supported non-treed) and
`T13_part_polygons_epsg8058.gpkg` (**118**, all non-treed). **The 38 treed and minor-unit
areas have no polygons anywhere**, so M1's "every area appears" could not be drawn from an
existing layer.

**Resolved by deriving all 156 from the census cells** — the same cells the scatters
aggregate — by the technique UNZONED v3 validated at zero mismatches. That is deliberate
and not merely expedient: mixing a census-derived class with a vector-derived class would
put **two unit definitions under one legend**. One source, one definition, and the
geometry closes to 0.005 ha.

**D2 · §4's hectare figure conflates two populations.** The spec says *"54 of 93 supported
patches drop at that floor, but only 570 of 12,048 hectares go with them."* Both numbers
are real and they do not belong to the same sentence:

| statement | correct figure |
|---|---|
| the **54 supported** tracts that drop at the floor carry… | **474.4 ha** of the 11,952.8 ha supported |
| all unzoned ground **minus** the 39 plotted tracts is… | **569.8 ha** of 12,048.1 ha |

The second is larger because it includes the **532 tracts that were never supported at
all**. M2's face now carries both, each with its own denominator.

**D3 · 22% of the property carries no vegetation-community mapping — found by drawing it.**
The property boundary encloses **85,910.8 ha**; the 156 zoned areas and 625 unzoned tracts
together cover **67,247.3 ha**. The remaining **18,663.4 ha — 22%** has no community label
and enters **no analysis anywhere in this project**. This is the mapped-vs-true-farm
denominator distinction, and no scatter can show it because a scatter has no blank space.
**On a map it is a fifth of the page**, and my first M1 caption wrongly attributed all the
white to unzoned country. Every map now names it.

### M5 · the unzoned country on its own terms

**The four classes verified against the geopackage before drawing**, same discipline as
§3's:

| class | n | ha | design seat | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| plotted — 500 cells or more | **39** | **11,478.3** | 39 / 11,478.3 | OK |
| supported, under the floor | **54** | **474.4** | 54 / 474.4 | OK |
| never supported | **532** | **95.3** | 532 / 95.4 | **95.3, a rounding difference — both reported** |
| **total** | **625** | **12,048.1** | 625 / 12,048.1 | OK |

**These four rows resolve D2 permanently.** A count and an area with different
denominators in one sentence caused it; four rows each carrying its own count *and* its
own area cannot. **586 of 625 tracts are not plotted and they carry 570 of 12,048
hectares** — the 500-cell floor removes most of the tracts and almost none of the ground.
The whole never-supported tail is **under 100 ha across 532 tracts**: a few large tracts
and a long tail of slivers, which is the shape of the country rather than a shortcoming of
the threshold.

**The fifteen standard-grazing plots are marked as locations and given no value** (C10).
They are plot support (~1 ha) on a pixel-support map, and the face says so in a clause:
measured on the ground at one-hectare scale while everything coloured is measured from
satellite on a 25 m grid, and the two scales are never mixed. This is the only map in the
project where they have somewhere to sit.

**Verified on the rendered file:** background is white and not transparent; the faintest
class (community hue at 0.30) is distinguishable from the paddock context outlines, and no
grey fill class appears on this map for it to collide with.

**Five defects were on rendered faces and none showed in an exit code** (I-60). The PNGs
were written with a **transparent background** — `theme_void()` sets none, and a
transparent map dropped into a slide renders black or shows whatever is behind it. The
scale bar sat on top of the polygons. M2's legend carried a swatch with no data. M1's
footnote claimed *"nothing is left off this map"* while 22% of the property was left off.
Both ramps started at near-white, so the driest country was indistinguishable from
no-data — the opposite of what the grey class exists to say. All fixed and re-verified by
opening the files.

**Labels: none registered, and that is the intended outcome.** M3 and M4 reuse the
canonical labels verbatim — *Share of the area's cells seen wet, mean over years (%)* and
*5th-percentile ground cover, mean of cells (%)* — so a reader moving between a scatter
and a map meets one vocabulary (EC). No new quantity is introduced, so the caption
register needs no entry.

**Issued in response to this run, and written to `docs/Gayini_issues_log.md`:**
**EQ** — any client-facing statement of extent names the analysed area and the property
area together, or names neither; *"across Gayini"* without a denominator is not available.
It governs the report stream and the article, not a re-render of these maps, which already
carry it. Filed alongside it: **a map with no background is a map that arrives black** —
`theme_void()` sets none, `ggsave()` writes transparent, and only opening the file in its
destination context tells you.

**Carried for the article, not written here.** *The standard-grazing arm was never
reportable above plot support because it had no paddock to belong to.* M5 is the only map
in the project where that arm has somewhere to sit, and the fifteen plots are the join
between it and every plot-support result. Worth a sentence in the article; recorded here so
it is not rediscovered.

**Cultural sensitivity.** No place is labelled on any face. Vegetation community names
follow existing report-stream usage exactly and no new naming is introduced. These are the
most directly place-showing artefacts the project has produced and they are Council-facing;
nothing on them names a location the report stream does not already name.
