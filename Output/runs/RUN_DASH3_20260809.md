## 2 · EC — the shared label

**Chosen:**

| | |
|---|---|
| dashboards, response panel | **"5th-percentile ground cover, per cell (%)"** |
| TEMPORAL-1 | **"5th-percentile ground cover, mean of cells (%)"** |

**Both applied in this pass, so the two arrive as a matched pair.** Registered in the
caption register as the canonical wording, with four other axis labels.

*"Poorest seasons" is gone* — it was interpretive, which EC forbids, and you were right
that it is part of why the panel is hard to explain.

**They share a stem rather than being byte-identical, deliberately.** The dashboards plot
individual cells; TEMPORAL-1 plots the mean of a unit's cells. Labelling both *"mean per
cell"* would make the dashboard axis false. Naming the **aggregation** while keeping the
**quantity** identical satisfies EC and stays true to what each axis shows — the divergence
EC exists to prevent is in the quantity, and that is now one wording.
