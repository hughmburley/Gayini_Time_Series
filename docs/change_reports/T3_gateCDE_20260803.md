## The headline: the pre-registered falsification condition fired, and it was honoured

T3 v3 wrote the rule before the numbers: *"If area is highly sensitive across plausible thresholds with no natural break, 'refugia' is a chosen cut on a continuum and must be reported as a gradient surface, not an area figure."*

Gate B1 measured exactly that. **No headline threshold has been set, and none will be.** `is_selected_threshold` is populated only as `selection_role = 'operational_lidar_input'`, never as a headline.

**This is the strongest available evidence that the project's decision rules are not fitted after the fact.** The rule was written on 27 July, the numbers arrived on 3 August, and they went against the category the rule was protecting. Nothing was tuned to rescue it.

### Four schema decisions beyond the spec, each with a reason the project has already paid for

- **`grid_epsg`** — the spec carries `pixel_area_ha` and `scope_filter_sql` because v1's failures were an unstated constant and an unstated scope. **D8 was an unstated grid.** Same class, same fix.
- **`measurement_basis`** — `'measured'` vs `'reprojected_for_overlay'`. Without it, someone quotes 3,744 ha as the green-share area and re-opens D8. The green-share 8058 rows carry the full warning string in the column itself.
- **`selection_role`** — so `is_selected_threshold` reads as `operational_lidar_input` and cannot be mistaken for a headline.
- **`scope` enum extended** with `farm_boundary_native` for the one row carrying the **measured** green-share number on its own grid. The native farm mask (86,384.97 ha implied) is **not** the census mapped footprint (67,349.332 ha) — genuinely different scopes, which is why it needed its own label rather than being forced into `all_pixel`.

**Threshold selection differs by metric, deliberately.** Total-cover takes **75** (the design-seat operational primary). Green-share takes **50**, because "majority green" is 50 *by definition of majority* — Task M's definitional cut, and the one the entire reconciliation rests on. Setting green-share to 75 would have invented a number. Flagged rather than assumed.

### The substantive test — Gate E point 3

**Persistent ground is roughly twice as flood-exposed as non-treed ground generally, and the result is threshold-independent.**

| surface | area-weighted mean flood freq | vs baseline |
|---|---|---|
| non_treed baseline (988,831 px) | **23.32%** | — |
| total-cover ≥ 75 | **48.49%** | 2.08× |
| total-cover ≥ 79 | **50.13%** | 2.15× |
| total-cover ≥ 82 | **46.31%** | 1.99× |
| green-share ≥ 50 | **57.00%** | 2.44× |

**This is exactly the test Decision 1 designed.** The result holds at all three total-cover cuts, so it is a property of the country and not of the cut. Had it held at only one, the overlay would have been measuring the threshold.

**The exceptions, stated rather than rounded away.** 12 of the 257 components **do** fall below the baseline — 5 total-cover components at t=75 (minimum 13.32%) and 7 green-share components (minimum 6.94%). They are all small and together hold **144.59 ha of 14,844.69 ha, 0.97% of persistent area**. At t=79 and t=82 none fall below. The below-baseline green-share patches are "green but dry" and are **not explained here**.

*(An earlier render of the Gate E figure claimed "not one of 257 components falls below the baseline". That was wrong — derived from the area-weighted means rather than the per-component minima, and visibly contradicted by its own scatter. Corrected in the figure, the caption and here.)*

**Distance to channel: ABSENT** (T3-I5). No channel or watercourse layer is registered or present anywhere. The only hydrological geometry is `irrigation_bank_cuts` — Task J irrigation infrastructure, not natural channel; substituting it would be a category error. **Mean flood frequency is a proxy, and the channel-association claim remains proxy-based.** Gate B2's dendritic map is consistent with it but is not itself evidence, per the design-seat instruction not to carry the pattern forward as a finding.

**Reference-paddock enrichment is flagged, not reported.** 22 of 40 green-share components sit >50% inside a reference paddock against a reference share of ~11% of mapped area. All four reference zones are Bala, so treatment is perfectly nested within block, and L-01 applies. **This is not a management effect and must not be read as one.**

## Audits requested at Gate D

**Checksums >50 MB — known-DIRTY on 2 of 7.** `raster_total_veg_annual_mean_8058` (609.2 MB) and `raster_total_veg_annual_jjason_8058` (579.6 MB) carry **whole-file** SHA-256; a canonical verifier reports drift on unchanged files. The other five are correct. **Not fixed** — rewriting a registered checksum wants sign-off. Logged T3-I3.

**`IS NULL` on float columns — known-CLEAN.** Two independent reasons: SQLite cannot store NaN (the Python driver converts to NULL, verified directly; four live fact tables show 0 non-finite among non-NULL), and the only parquet/duckdb site already pairs `IS NOT NULL` with `isfinite()`. The trap is confined to the parquet path. Logged T3-I2 as a standing rule.

**Issues log:** T3-I1 through T3-I7 added, IDs checked against the on-disk log before use. T3-I1 (reprojection ordering) is logged as a **general rule**, not a T3 note.
