## Gate B1 — full sweep and break characterisation

`scripts/05_ground_cover/T3_gateB1_threshold_sweep.py` → `Output/tables/T3_gateB1_{threshold_sweep,elasticity,p05_upper_tail}.csv` (102 sweep rows). `Output/` is gitignored; the script and the `figure_asset` rows are the committed record.

Sweep: `veg_p05` 40→90 step 1, `non_treed` and `all_pixel` separately, using `SCOPE_NON_TREED` / `SCOPE_ALL_PIXEL` and `PIXEL_AREA_HA`. Per threshold: pixel count, area, both bases (never rebased), mean and median flood frequency, components ≥ 5 ha. **The NaN-aware predicate from Gate A F2 is used throughout.**

**Acceptance check — the Context table reproduces exactly:**

| p05 ≥ | pixels | area (ha) | spec | mean ff | components ≥5 ha |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 656,536 | **40,935.96** | 40,935.96 ✓ | 29.81 | 172 |
| 65 | 321,499 | **20,045.92** | 20,045.92 ✓ | 38.38 | 156 |
| 75 | 133,123 | **8,300.41** | 8,300.41 ✓ | 47.86 | 75 |
| 80 | 67,028 | **4,179.29** | 4,179.29 ✓ | 49.34 | 82 |

v1's values (41,338 / 20,339 / 8,355 / 4,193) are not reproduced. `all_pixel` ≥50 → 46,414.90 ha and ≥80 → 5,515.73 ha, both matching the spec.

### 1. There is no natural break — the decline is smooth

Elasticity (% of area lost per +1 pp, central difference of ln area, `non_treed`): **5.3 at t=60 · 8.1 at 65 · 8.7 at 70 · 8.0 at 75 · 13.5 at 78 · 22.5 at 80 · 40.4 at 82 · 69.0 at 84.** The second derivative of ln(area) stays within ±0.006 across 65–75 and then goes monotonically negative. No knee, no plateau, no bimodality.

**"Refugia" is a chosen cut on a continuum, and the manuscript must say so.** This is the honest answer to the spec's falsification test, and it is a reportable result rather than a failure.

### 2. The 80→85 collapse is the tail's shape — F11 confirmed and now positively explained

`veg_p05` above 75, `non_treed`, bin width 1:

| bin | 75 | 76 | 77 | 78 | 79 | **80** | **81** | 82 | 83 | 84 | 85 | 86 | 87 | 88 | 89 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| px | 10,829 | 11,459 | 13,150 | 14,740 | 15,917 | **17,161** | **17,259** | 14,918 | 10,691 | 5,297 | 1,417 | 235 | 43 | 7 | 0 |

Unimodal with the upper mode at **80–82**, thinning to zero by 89 against a non-treed maximum of **88.66**. The 4,179.29 → 106.12 ha drop is exactly the cumulative sum of that thinning. A saturation artefact piles up **at** the ceiling; this thins away from a mode well below it. **F11 stays withdrawn and is now positively explained rather than merely uncontradicted.**

> **The consequence outranks the explanation: a cut at 80 sits one unit from the modal bin — the point of maximum density, and so the least stable place on the entire curve to site a headline number.** Elasticity there is 22.5%/pp against 8.0%/pp at 75.

### 3. The flood-frequency peak is a plateau, and the naive argmax is noise

Mean flood frequency plateaus at ~49.5% across **t=77–81** (49.09 / 49.48 / **49.59** / 49.34 / 48.55), then declines — above ~81 the surface stops selecting for wetness. The unrestricted argmax is **80.41% at t=88, resting on seven pixels (0.4 ha)**. Restricting to thresholds holding ≥ 1,000 ha, the supported peak is **49.59% at t=79** (82,945 px, 5,171.7 ha, 85 components). The spec's "~78–80" is right in location; "plateau" is the accurate word.

### 4. Components, and the connectivity degree of freedom

Components ≥ 5 ha (`MIN_COMPONENT_PX = 81`, 8-connectivity): 172 at t=50 → 75 at t=75 → **85 at t=78–79** → 82 at t=80 → 57 at t=82 → 20 at t=84 → **1 at t=85** → 0 above.

**Queen vs rook differs by at most 2 patches at every candidate cut** (70: 125/124 · 75: 75/77 · 78: 85/87 · 80: 82/84 · 85: 1/1). Worth recording, because "how many refugia patches" is otherwise a silent free parameter.

### 5. Operational range for the LiDAR overlay — Gate D input, not a selection

t=85 yields 106 ha in **one** component: nothing to overlay against. t=50 yields 40,936 ha, 61% of mapped ground: too diffuse to mean anything. **The usable window is t ≈ 75–82** (2,033–8,300 ha, 57–85 components); within it **78–79** maximises component count while still sitting on the wetness plateau. **Stated as a range, not a choice.**

### The three numbers, and why only one is a measurement

| number | what it is | status |
|---|---|---|
| **6,457.95 ha** | 71,755 px × 0.09 ha at native 30 m EPSG:3577 | **the measurement** |
| 4,474.03 ha | the same native pixel *count* × the 8058 pixel area | an arithmetic conversion of a count, not a measurement of anything — this is D8 |
| **3,744.20 ha** | the actual bilinear reprojection to 8058, thresholded on the 8058 grid (60,050 px) | a genuine but different operation |

**This strengthens the D8 withdrawal.** 4,474.03 ha is not merely the wrong grid for the measurement — **it is not even what a correct reprojection produces.** Thresholding after bilinear smoothing is not the same operation as smoothing after thresholding, and the two differ by 16%. Reported unadjusted; nothing was tuned to reconcile them. **The measured area is the native one, and the 8058 raster is a reprojection for overlay, not a re-measurement** — stated verbatim in the registered `legend_semantics`.
